### 🎯 Business Problem & Solution Managing domestic chemical sourcing alongside international imports (FOB Sea China, CIF Mumbai, Ex-Works) 
requires strict tracking of vendor pricing, fill rates, and shipping documents (Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, COA/MSDS) to avoid heavy port demurrage charges at *Nhava Sheva (JNPT).  I built an automated
**Excel Procurement & Import Control Tower* that cleans raw PO logs via *Power Query* and surfaces live commercial KPIs: 
* *Total Procurement Spend Monitored:* ₹8.61+ Crore across 30+ domestic and international chemical orders (DMAP, HATU, Benzoic Acid). * *Negotiated Cost Savings:* ₹2.96+ Lakhs saved against historical CRM/ERP rate benchmarks. * *Supplier Fill Rate & OTIF:* Achieved *97.8% Vendor Fill Rate* across approved Indian manufacturers and Chinese exporters. * *Proactive Nhava Sheva Demurrage Alerts:* Built live TODAY() SLA formulas flagging pending Bills of Lading *3 days prior to vessel arrival* for immediate CHA/EXIM action.  ---
